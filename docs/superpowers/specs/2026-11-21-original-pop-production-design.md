The user authorized a general-purpose upgrade for today's presentation. Beauty and a Beat and Shape of You are production benchmarks only. The implementation decision is to extend the current strict note-conditioned engine and its real Web Audio renderer. A text-only generator cannot guarantee the melody; the optional local MusicGen adapter remains an unverified preview because its chroma conditioning cannot enforce exact notes. No neural inference or commercial mastering quality will be claimed without evidence.

Add a fresh-input melody character summary (range, density, rhythm/tempo evidence, motion and phrases). Tempo is an estimate with confidence and does not retime the captured melody. Add optional production auto/dance-pop/percussive-pop settings through existing producer, server and persistence paths. Auto follows the chosen genre; explicit instrument exclusions take priority.

Dance-pop uses steady kicks, bright chord articulation, evolving density and bass/kick separation. Percussive pop uses a restrained pluck texture, syncopation, lighter percussion and space around the lead. These are original generic production choices, informed by producer interviews, not transcriptions. Harmony and register respond to the actual input notes, with economical voice movement and lower density under busy melodies. Phrase-aware development affects backing only.

Keep legacy settings compatible and support all existing genres. Use deterministic synthesis, separate dry lead routing, bounded backing stereo and distinct original synthesized percussion; no external samples, model downloads, copied hooks or copyrighted note sequences.
